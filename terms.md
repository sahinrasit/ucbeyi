  Town era, appearance only). The subscription sells no power and never locks free content or packs you bought.
- **Length and price:** monthly (1 month). The price is shown in your local currency on the Treasury's Berat tab and in the store's

- **Automatic renewal:** the subscription renews automatically for the same length and price unless cancelled **at least 24 hours before**

  changes, the store informs you in advance and asks for your consent where required.
- **Manage and cancel:** you can manage or cancel at any time. On iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions; on Google Play: Play Store →

  the subscription. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current period; the subscription stays active until then. Refunds for unused
  time follow the store's rules.
- **When it ends:** berat stipends, Hâs rewards and akçe you already received stay with you; the Parade Outfit is removed (unless bought

- **Restore:** on another device or after reinstalling, with the same store account, the Treasury's **"Restore purchases"** button restores
