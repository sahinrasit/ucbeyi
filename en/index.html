Your guide

Lala tells the tale, you walk the road

Lala

Welcome, my bey. The elders set you at the head of this camp, and it fell to me, Lala, to take your hand. Sit by the fire: first I will tell you the story everyone on this frontier knows, then we will build your realm together.

Lala is the young bey’s (or hatun’s) tutor and guide in the game, a fictional character. He walks you through your first steps, tells you where to tap and whom to heed at the Divan, and in the evenings reads the history of the House of Osman with you, page by page.

Palace, kitchen and festivals

Set the table, raise the screen

Ahmed IIIThe state works in the tulip garden and at the table too. Hold your festival, but hear the bazaar’s troubles.
  • Cook and host banquets in the imperial kitchen (Matbah-ı Âmire).

How to play

Your first days

  1. Found your realm

    Lala greets you at the camp. Choose your name and your banner and name your beylik.

  2. Gather resources

    Send workers to the forest, the quarry and the mine for wood, stone and iron. Taxes pile up in your chest; collect them and feed your people.

  3. Upgrade and advance

    Tap a building and “Yükselt” (upgrade). As your palace grows, your realm moves from Camp to Village, Town, City and Capital, and each era opens new buildings and offices.

  4. Raise an army, march

    Recruit at the barracks. Campaigns open only against cities on your border; compare your strength with the garrison, deploy in three lanes and two lines, or ask Lala for advice.

  5. Read the reigns, decide

    Each sultan’s reign is a chapter of events and decisions. Balance treasury, people, army and scholars; after each key choice, see what really happened.

    Lala

    In the evenings we read the history of the House of Osman page by page: on every page you take a ruler’s place, decide, and see what history did.

Purchases

An open ledger

No power for sale

No ads, no loot boxes. Resources and provisions are not sold. Prices are shown in your local currency in the store and in the game’s Treasury before you buy.

Included

  • The first seven reigns, from the founding to Mehmed the Conqueror.
  • City building and the five eras, the conquest map, famous battles, the sea, the Divan, diplomacy, the kitchen, the coffeehouse, festivals and every side game.
  • The first three events of every locked reign. The main story can be finished without any purchase.

Optional

  • Era packs (Rise, Transformation, Reform) or all of them as the Full Sultanate; bought once, restorable.
  • Outfits: looks only. Akçe purses: measured in days of your era’s income.
  • Hanedan Beratı, a monthly subscription: 8% of your era’s daily income each day, the Hâs path of the Ferman book, a 15-minute free speed-up window and the Alay outfit. Renews automatically; cancel any time in your store settings.

Support & FAQ in English · Privacy · Terms

How do I restore my purchases?

Open the Treasury (Hazine) and tap “Satın almaları geri yükle” (Restore purchases) while signed in to the same store account. Permanent items and the Hanedan Beratı subscription come back; akçe purses are consumable and are not restored.

How do I cancel the subscription?

iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Android: Play Store → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; deleting the app does not cancel it.

Refunds

Handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play (order history).
