# Privacy Policy — Uç Beyi: Osmanlı Strateji

[Türkçe](../gizlilik/) · [Terms of Use](../terms/) · [Support](../#english)

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026<br>
**Publisher (data controller):** Raşit Şahin, Türkiye<br>
**Contact:** <semasahin3527@gmail.com>

This policy explains what information the mobile game Uç Beyi: Osmanlı Strateji (titled Devlet-i Aliyye in the game; the "Game") keeps,
where, and with whom it is shared. In short: the Game **has no accounts**, **runs no server of its own**, **contains no advertising** and
**does not track you** (it uses no advertising ID). Your save stays on your device. What leaves your device: purchase information sent to the
payment providers (RevenueCat, Apple, Google) so that in-app purchases work; **anonymous gameplay data** to improve the Game (Google Firebase
Analytics; you can turn it off in the Game); and, if you allowed notifications, a **notification token** so announcements can reach you
(Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).

## 1. Information kept on your device

- **Game save.** Your realm, reigns, progress, settings, names you enter (such as your realm's name) and the in-game result of your
  purchases (unlocked packs, outfits, the ledger of purchased akçe) are stored in your device's local storage. They are not sent to us or to
  anyone else; we cannot see them.
- **Device backup.** On iPhone the save may be included in your iCloud device backup if enabled; on Android it may be included in Google's
  automatic app backup if enabled. These backups are Apple's and Google's own services under their terms; the Game sends them nothing.
- **Save backup code.** The "Backup" section of the Bey's Ledger turns your save into a text code. The code leaves your device only if
  **you** share it; the Game never sends it anywhere. The code contains your whole save (including names you entered): keep it somewhere safe.
- **Local purchase counters.** Counters such as how often the Treasury was opened stay on your device and are never sent anywhere.

If you delete the app, the save on your device is deleted too (it cannot be recovered without a backup or code).

## 2. Notifications

If you allow it, the Game shows **local notifications** (for example when a building is finished), scheduled on your device. If you allowed
notifications the Game can also receive **announcements** (remote push; e.g. a new chapter or a seasonal event). For this **Google Firebase
Cloud Messaging** (Google LLC / Google Ireland Ltd.) gives your device a random **notification token**, and the Game subscribes to these
topics: announcements for everyone (`hepsi`) and your realm's age (e.g. `cag_koy`). Announcements are sent to these topics; no personal
message, name or save data is sent or used. You can turn them off at any time in your device Settings or with the "All notifications" switch
in Künye → Notifications; when the switch is off the Game leaves every topic.

## 3. In-app purchases and RevenueCat

Payment is always taken by **Apple (App Store)** or **Google (Google Play)**; your card and bank details never reach us or RevenueCat.
To verify and restore purchases the Game uses **RevenueCat, Inc.** (USA):

- The Game connects to RevenueCat with an **anonymous user ID** (a random identifier generated by RevenueCat on your device). Your name,
  email, phone number and in-game names are **not** sent; the Game sets no user attributes, advertising or attribution identifiers.
- Information sent to and kept by RevenueCat: purchase and subscription history (product, date, price and currency, transaction identifiers,
  subscription status), the store's purchase receipt, the store country and technical information such as app, operating-system and device
  type; and, as a consequence of the connection, your IP address. It is used only to deliver and restore what you bought, to know whether a
  subscription is active and to prevent fraud.
- The "Support ID" at the bottom of the Treasury screen is the last eight characters of this anonymous ID. If you give it to us we can find
  your purchase record together; otherwise we cannot identify you.
- RevenueCat's own practices: [RevenueCat privacy policy](https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy). Apple and Google process payments under their
  own privacy policies.

## 4. Gameplay data (Google Firebase Analytics)

To improve the Game (to see which chapter is too hard or which screen goes unused) the Game uses **Google Firebase Analytics** (Google LLC /
Google Ireland Ltd.; processor). What is sent is **anonymous**:

- **In-game interaction:** screens opened; founding the realm, age-ups, building levels, city conquests, sieges, battle and mini-game results
  (mode, place, score, won/lost), chapter start, the ID of the choice made and chapter end, badges earned; opening the Treasury, tapping a
  product and the purchase outcome (product ID; the Game sends no price); the notification-permission answer and notification taps.
- **Summary properties:** age, palace level, furthest chapter, number of packs owned, days-since-install range, notification-permission state.
- **Collected automatically by Firebase:** a random app-instance ID, session start and duration, first open, app version, operating system,
  device model, language and coarse location derived from the IP address (country/city; Google Analytics does not store the IP address
  itself), and a summary of store purchases (`in_app_purchase`).

Not sent: the **advertising ID** (IDFA on iOS, advertising ID on Android; the app does not read them and does not ask for App Tracking
Transparency permission), your realm's or Bey's name or any text you typed, the save backup code, email, phone. The data is **not used for
advertising or tracking** and is not shared with advertising partners; data-sharing options in the Firebase console are off.

**Turning it off:** switch off **"Oyun verisi paylaş" (Share gameplay data)** in Künye → Notifications and the Game sends no events and
Firebase collection is disabled on your device (the setting survives restarts). The switch is on by default.

Google's practices: [Firebase privacy and security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy).

## 5. What we do not collect

The Game does **not** collect or use: account or login details, name, email, phone, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera,
advertising ID; advertising tools; crash-reporting services; there is no tracking (as defined by Apple). The Game has no chat and no
user-generated content sharing.

## 6. International transfer

Purchase information is processed on RevenueCat's systems in the United States; gameplay data and the notification token on Google's systems
(USA and EU). This information is processed only for the purposes described above, by the service providers named in this policy
(RevenueCat, Google), some of which are located outside Türkiye.

## 7. Retention

The save on your device remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Purchase records at RevenueCat are kept for as long as RevenueCat's
and the stores' legal and accounting obligations require. Event data in Google Analytics is deleted after 2 months. The notification token
stops working when the app is uninstalled.

## 8. Your rights

Turkish Law No. 6698 (Art. 11) and the law of your country (for example the GDPR in the EU) give you rights such as access, correction,
deletion and objection. Your on-device save is only yours: you can delete it in the game or by uninstalling the app. For the purchase record
at RevenueCat, write to <semasahin3527@gmail.com> with your "Support ID" and we will forward your request to RevenueCat. Gameplay data is
anonymous, so we cannot find you in it; you can stop collection with the "Share gameplay data" switch and invalidate the app-instance ID by
uninstalling. The store's own record of your purchases is held in your Apple or Google account. You may also complain to the competent
data-protection authority.

## 9. Children

The Game is designed for ages 13 and up and is not offered in a children's category. In-app purchases are subject to the store's parental
controls (Apple "Ask to Buy", Google Play parental approval). If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, write to
<semasahin3527@gmail.com>.

## 10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published at the same address with a new effective date. If the kind of information collected
changes (for example a new service is added), we will also say so in that update's store release notes.
